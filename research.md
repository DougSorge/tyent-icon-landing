## How it works (water path)
1. **Inlet** — tap/diverter feeds the unit; source-water sensing stops flow if supply drops; temperature sensor blocks hot water
2. **Filtration** — 2 Ultra Plus filters: activated carbon (chlorine, VOCs, odor) + UF membrane (fine particles, bacteria), keeps minerals; tested against 200+ contaminants; RFID recognition + life %
3. **Hydrogen Booster Cell** — dedicated H₂ generation, independent of pH
4. **Ionizing Cell** — electrolysis over platinum-coated titanium mesh plates; membrane splits stream into alkaline (OH⁻, cathode) and acidic (H⁺, anode); pH 1.7–12.5, ORP to –1150 mV
5. **Power** — 270W SMPS (HALO), stable current across settings
6. **Control** — touchscreen modes: ALK 1/2/3, PURIFY, H₂, ACD 1/2, TURBO; jog dial; preset volumes
7. **Dispense** — proximity sensor = hands-free; auto shutoff at preset volume; acidic water exits a secondary drain hose
8. **Self-care** — auto-clean cycles, guided descale, self-diagnosis, leak detection, eco/sleep mode
