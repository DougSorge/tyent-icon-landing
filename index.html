02 / Inlet

It checks the water before it starts.

Tap water comes in through a diverter. Before every pour, ICON checks the supply and protects itself.

  • Source-water sensing stops the flow when no supply is detected
  • A temperature sensor blocks hot water from reaching the cell
  • Leak detection and self-diagnosis run in the background
03 / Filtration

Two filters before any electricity.

Two Ultra Plus cartridges clean the water before it reaches the plates, and keep the minerals that help ionization.

  • 1st · Carbon: chlorine, VOCs, odor and taste
  • 2nd · UF membrane: fine particles and bacteria
  • Reduces lead, pesticides and pharmaceutical residues

04 / Dual-cell core

Two cells. Independent control.

In older ionizers, hydrogen only rose with the alkaline setting. Fusion separates the two jobs.

  • Hydrogen Booster Cell: generates molecular hydrogen without changing pH
  • Ionizing Cell: sets the pH, from strong acidic to strong alkaline
  • Run either one, or both together for alkaline water with maximum hydrogen
05 / Electrolysis

Platinum on titanium, current through water.

Platinum-coated titanium mesh plates pass current through the water. A membrane splits the stream in two: the cathode side becomes alkaline and hydrogen-rich, and the acidic side drains away.
